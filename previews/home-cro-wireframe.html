05  COVERAGE — the missing block

Where I work

Law firm and local SEO across Kansas, Texas, Michigan, Louisiana, Ohio, Pennsylvania, California, Arizona and New Mexico — one client per city.

US map, states with a live city page filled
each state links to its hub
Kansas — 8 citiesTexas — 3 Michigan — 2Louisiana — 2 OhioPennsylvaniaCalifornia ArizonaNew Mexico + your city — ask
The single biggest SEO gap on the page. Twenty city pages exist and the homepage body links to none of them — only the footer and one service card do. The homepage is the strongest page on the site; this block passes that strength to the money pages and makes the “across US states” claim verifiable instead of asserted.
Honesty guard: the words must match the map. Nine states have pages today, so the copy says nine states, not “all 50”. The dashed “+ your city” pill is the honest way to invite the rest.
